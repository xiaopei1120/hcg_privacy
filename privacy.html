每日攒（DailySave） 由个人开发者 杨桐（下称「我们」）运营。本政策说明我们如何处理与本应用相关的信息。

1. 我们不收集的内容

我们不运营自建用户账号系统，也不通过自有服务器收集、存储或出售你的存钱记录、心愿清单或身份信息。本应用不连接银行或支付账户，不会读取短信、通讯录；不会在未使用系统文件选择器的情况下访问你的文件；不做广告追踪，不使用第三方统计分析 SDK。

2. 本机保存的信息

你在应用内输入或生成的数据默认保存在本设备，包括但不限于：

  • 存入/取出流水、余额与每日目标
  • 心愿名称、金额、状态、图标等
  • 提醒开关与时间、界面语言、主题/皮肤/图标偏好
  • 完整版权益的本地缓存（以 Apple StoreKit 为准）
  • 小组件/灵动岛所需的进度快照（经系统 App Group 与主应用共享，仍在你的设备上）

卸载应用通常会删除本机数据。请在卸载或换机前自行导出备份。

3. 备份文件

若你购买完整版，可以自行导出/导入备份文件。导入会整库替换本机数据。请自行保管备份文件；通过邮件、网盘等发出即离开本应用控制范围。

4. 系统权限与系统服务

仅在功能需要时请求，你可在系统设置中关闭：

能力用途
通知可选的每日存钱提醒（本地通知，无远程推送）
Face ID / 设备密码查看你设为隐藏的心愿；生物特征由系统处理，我们不采集、不上传人脸或指纹
网络内购、恢复购买；核心记账可离线使用

购买与收据校验由 Apple StoreKit / App Store 完成。我们不获知完整银行卡号。Apple 可能按自身规则处理交易与设备信息。

5. 处理目的与法律依据

本机数据由你创建，用于提供存钱习惯与心愿记录。内购处理基于履行合同及正当利益（提供你选择的外观与备份权益）。你可随时关闭提醒或卸载应用。

6. 保留与删除

  • 本机：删除对应记录、卸载应用，或按系统方式清除 App 数据。
  • App Store 交易记录由 Apple 保留。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 周岁以下儿童（或你所在地更高年龄门槛）收集个人信息。若你认为儿童向我们提交了信息，请按下方联系方式告知，我们将配合处理本应用可控范围内的数据。

8. 你的权利

在本应用可控范围内，你可通过应用内编辑/删除记录、导出备份或卸载应用来访问和删除数据。欧盟/英国等用户亦可按适用法律向监管机构投诉。涉及 Apple 账户或内购，请通过 Apple 支持行使权利。

9. 政策更新

更新后会修订本页日期；重大变更可能通过应用内或商店说明提示。继续使用视为已了解更新后的政策。

DailySave (“the App”) is operated by Yang Tong (“we”, “us”), an individual developer. This policy describes how information related to the App is handled.

1. What we do not collect

We do not run our own user accounts or backend that stores your ledger or wishes. We do not sell your data. The App does not connect to bank or payment accounts, and does not use advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not read SMS or contacts, and we only access files you pick through the system file picker for backup import/export.

2. Data stored on your device

By default, content you enter is stored on device, including:

  • Deposits, withdrawals, balance, and daily goals
  • Wish-list names, amounts, status, and icons
  • Reminder and language settings, theme / skin / icon preferences
  • A local cache of Full Unlock status (StoreKit is authoritative)
  • Widget and Live Activity snapshots shared via the system App Group—still on your devices

Uninstalling the App typically deletes on-device data. Export a backup before uninstalling or changing devices.

3. Backup files

If you purchase Full Unlock, you may export/import a backup file. Import replaces local data. You are responsible for files you share outside the App.

4. System permissions and services

FeaturePurpose
NotificationsOptional local daily reminders (no remote push)
Face ID / device passcodeReveal wishes you hid; biometrics stay in the system—we do not collect or upload Face ID or fingerprints
NetworkIn-app purchase and restore; core bookkeeping works offline

Purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit / the App Store. We do not receive full payment-card numbers.

5. Purpose and legal bases

On-device data is created by you to provide saving and wish features. Purchases are processed to perform the contract you request and for our legitimate interests in delivering those features. You may disable reminders or uninstall the App.

6. Retention and deletion

  • On device: delete records in the App, uninstall, or clear App data in system settings.
  • App Store transaction records are retained by Apple.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or a higher age required where you live). Contact us if you believe a child has provided information we control.

8. Your rights

You can access and delete data we control by editing records, exporting a backup, or uninstalling. You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority where applicable. For Apple ID or purchases, use Apple Support.

9. Changes

We will update the date on this page. Continued use after changes means you have been informed of the updated policy.
